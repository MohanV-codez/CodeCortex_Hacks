## 🚀 Overview

**FinSafe** is an AI/ML-powered financial safety platform designed for **everyday users, small merchants, shop owners, and department-store owners**.

Instead of treating fraud detection and investment risk as separate problems, FinSafe combines them into a **single Financial Health Score (0–100)**.

Users can simulate an investment and immediately see:

> **"How will investing ₹X affect my overall financial safety?"**

---

## 🎯 Problem

Existing financial tools often separate:

- Fraud detection
- Personal financial health
- Investment analysis

This makes it difficult for ordinary users to understand how a financial decision affects their **overall risk**.
